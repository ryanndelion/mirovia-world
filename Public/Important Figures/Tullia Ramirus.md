Once a lowly deckhand scrubbing air-ferry toilets, Tullia rose above her station and eventually worked her way to owning Celestial Horizons, an air-ferry company that specializes in hosting high-profile guests in luxury. She has lived through the worst that Nionosea and KiBiVi has to offer, and wants to change things for the better in the city. When the party first met her, she was still trying to gain leverage in the Senate. Thanks to the party's efforts and her regular public appearances, she quickly became a symbol for protecting the common-folk, garnering significant support among the under-represented in Nionosea such as the Rana and Awakened. In the most recent public elections, she finally got a seat in the Senate, representing KiBiVi.
